Applesauce Studios

Applesauce Studios is a premier comic book packaging studio and independent imprint founded by writer and creator Joseph Cacchiani. Built on the classic studio tradition, Applesauce Studios serves as the creative engine for Cacchiani’s original scripts and graphic fiction. Operating as a unified bullpen, the studio collaborates with a handpicked roster of exciting new talent and experienced working professionals who are already making their mark in the comic book industry. Together with this elite team of illustrators, colorists, and letterers, Applesauce Studios transforms high concept, character driven narratives into beautifully realized, high end sequential art.
